06 · SIGNAL VALIDATION

Does Cluster Buying Actually Work?

For every cluster-buy event in the historical record, we measured the 180-day forward return on the stock and compared it to SPY over the same window. The excess return is alpha. The hit rate is the share of signals that beat the benchmark.
